**What is Cloud Technology?**

Cloud technology refers to the delivery of computing services such as storage, processing power, networking, and software over the internet. This approach allows organizations to access and utilize resources as needed without investing in physical hardware. Cloud technology offers numerous benefits, including:

- **Scalability**: Easily scale resources up or down based on demand.

- **Cost Efficiency**: Pay for resources on a usage basis, avoiding large capital expenditures.

- **Accessibility**: Access data and applications from anywhere with an internet connection.

- **Enhanced Security**: Utilize advanced security measures provided by cloud service providers.

The evolution of cloud technology has transformed traditional IT practices, leading to innovations in various sectors, including finance, healthcare, and entertainment.
