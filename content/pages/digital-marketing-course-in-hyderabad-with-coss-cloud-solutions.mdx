**Basics of Digital Marketing**

- Introduction to digital marketing, understanding different online marketing channels, and the role of digital marketing in modern business.

**Website Planning and Development**

- Introduction to website creation, basic WordPress setup, and SEO-friendly website design principles to improve user engagement and search engine rankings.

- Learning on-page and off-page SEO techniques.

- Key aspects include keyword research, optimizing content, backlinking strategies, and using tools like Google Search Console.

**Google Ads and Pay-Per-Click (PPC) Advertising**

- Basics of creating and managing Google Ads campaigns.

- Understanding keywords, ad placements, and managing PPC budgets for targeted advertising.

**Social Media Marketing (SMM)**

- Introduction to social media marketing on platforms like Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Twitter.

- Strategies for building an online brand presence, running effective ad campaigns, and engaging with audiences.

**Content Marketing and Blogging**

- Importance of quality content in digital marketing.

- Creating blog posts, using content calendars, and aligning content with SEO for improved visibility and engagement.

- Crafting email campaigns, segmenting email lists, and using automation tools like Mailchimp for personalized email marketing.

**Google Analytics and Data Interpretation**

- Setting up and using Google Analytics to track user behavior.

- Basics of affiliate marketing, setting up affiliate programs, and leveraging influencers for brand promotion and wider reach.
