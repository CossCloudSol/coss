**Azure Certification Paths Explained**

Azure certifications are structured to cater to different expertise levels:

- **Fundamental Level**: *Microsoft Certified: Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)*, suitable for beginners.

- **Associate Level**: *Azure Administrator Associate (AZ-104)* and *Azure Developer Associate (AZ-204)* for those with some cloud experience.

- **Expert Level**: *Azure Solutions Architect Expert (AZ-305)* and *DevOps Engineer Expert (AZ-400)*, targeted at experienced professionals.
