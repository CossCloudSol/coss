### **Course Overview — What the Full Stack Power BI Curriculum Covers**

This full-stack Power BI program takes learners through the complete analytics workflow:

**Data ingestion & preparation**

Connecting to Excel, CSV, SQL, Azure sources.

Power Query transformations (cleaning, merging, shaping).

**Data modeling & DAX**

Building star schemas, relationships, and implementing row-level security.

DAX fundamentals: calculated columns, measures, time intelligence, and performance tuning.

**Visualization & design**

Designing interactive reports using drill-throughs, bookmarks, custom visuals, and UX best practices.

**Deployment & governance**

Publishing to Power BI Service, workspace management, sharing, and embedding.

Introduction to Power BI Premium capabilities and refresh strategies.

**Advanced topics**

Integrating with Azure services, Power Automate flows, and basic Python/R integration for advanced analytics.
