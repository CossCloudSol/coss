### **What is Ethical Hacking?**

Ethical hacking, also known as penetration testing, involves identifying and fixing vulnerabilities in computer systems, networks, and applications to prevent cyberattacks. Unlike malicious hacking, ethical hacking is performed with legal permission and focuses on strengthening an organization&#8217;s security framework.

#### **Key Objectives of Ethical Hacking**

- Identifying system vulnerabilities before attackers do.

- Securing sensitive data against breaches.

- Enhancing overall network and system security.

#### **How It Differs from Malicious Hacking**

Ethical hacking is driven by a moral and legal framework, ensuring that the actions taken help protect systems rather than exploit them.

### **Why Ethical Hacking is Crucial in Today’s World**

With an exponential rise in cyberattacks worldwide, organizations are actively seeking professionals who can safeguard their digital assets. Ethical hackers play a pivotal role in mitigating risks and protecting businesses from data breaches.

#### **Legal and Ethical Aspects**

Ethical hackers adhere to strict regulations, working within the boundaries of the law to ensure that their actions benefit organizations without compromising privacy or integrity.
