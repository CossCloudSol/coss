**Introduction to Cloud Computing**

Cloud computing involves delivering computing services over the internet, including storage, processing power, databases, networking, and software. It allows businesses to avoid the expense of owning and maintaining physical servers, instead renting services from cloud providers like Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud.

Cloud computing has become integral in various industries, including healthcare, finance, e-commerce, and entertainment, due to its flexibility, scalability, and cost-effectiveness.
