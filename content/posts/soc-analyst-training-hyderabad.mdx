## What is a SOC Analyst?

A **SOC Analyst** is a cybersecurity expert responsible for monitoring, detecting, analyzing, and responding to security incidents in real time. SOC Analysts work in a **Security Operations Center (SOC)** and act as the first line of defense against cyber threats such as malware, phishing attacks, ransomware, and unauthorized access.
