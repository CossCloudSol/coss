**MySQL** is known for speed, simplicity, and reliability—perfect for web applications and startups.

**PostgreSQL** is feature-rich, open-source, and supports advanced analytics—ideal for enterprise-level data projects.

Professionals who understand both MySQL and PostgreSQL can easily adapt to diverse industry requirements.
