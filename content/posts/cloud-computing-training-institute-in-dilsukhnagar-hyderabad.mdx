### **What Is Cloud Computing? A Simple Overview for Beginners**

Cloud computing is the **delivery of computing services**—including servers, storage, databases, networking, and analytics—over the internet (“the cloud”). It eliminates the need for companies to own physical hardware and helps them scale faster.

#### **Types of Cloud Services: IaaS, PaaS, SaaS Explained**

**IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service):** Rent virtual machines and servers.

**PaaS (Platform as a Service):** Develop and deploy apps without managing infrastructure.

**SaaS (Software as a Service):** Access ready-to-use software applications online.

#### **Leading Cloud Platforms: AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud**

**Amazon Web Services (AWS)** – The most popular and widely used cloud platform.

**Microsoft Azure** – Known for enterprise integration and hybrid cloud solutions.

**Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** – Specializes in big data, AI, and machine learning workloads.
