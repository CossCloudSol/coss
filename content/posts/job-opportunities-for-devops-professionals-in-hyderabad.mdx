#### Why Choose DevOps?

DevOps is a set of practices that combines software development (Dev) and IT operations (Ops). It aims to shorten the development lifecycle and deliver high-quality software continuously. Companies are increasingly adopting DevOps to improve collaboration between development and operations teams, enhance productivity, and accelerate time-to-market.
